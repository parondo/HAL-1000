# Contributing

Write in English. Provide a small reproducible Python example, source version, mathematical contract and an independent test. Separate implementation defects from mathematical objections and new proposals.

Keep the research branch separate from main. Do not overwrite inputs or activate external methods without a documented compatibility review. Do not merge without the maintainer's approval. See AGENTS.md for typed contracts and STOP rules.

No new license declaration is made here; licensing remains a maintainer decision.
