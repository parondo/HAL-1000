# Research protocol and provenance

## Native source chain

Patrick Morcillo, Nyx corpus: https://www.fdaa.fr/
Scala Caelestis V4, Scriptura Pulveris; De Formis et Contactibus; De Referentiis Modularibus; De Praxi et Inferentia. Exact public section URLs and version snapshots still need to be pinned by the maintainer.
Historical structure-aware envelope operator: Morcillo, Structure-Aware LES via Envelope-Gated Subgrid Viscosity (LES-ACF), August 12, 2025, https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.5388953. This is a historical fluid-closure model, not a material fabrication law. Its percentile thresholds and legacy metrics are not imported.
Historical HAL-1000 architecture: https://github.com/parondo/HAL-1000

## External experimental references (verified publication links; no data imported)

- DISH, Sub-second volumetric 3D printing by synthesis of holographic light fields, Nature 650 (2026), https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10114-5. Code and example data: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17905914 and https://github.com/sugar10w/DISH. DISH uses digital **incoherent** synthesis of holographic light fields; this must not be identified with the coherent sum in P0.
- Wang et al., Stiffness control in dual color tomographic volumetric 3D printing, Nature Communications 13, 367 (2022), https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-28013-4. Relevant to spatial material-property control, not proof of Nyx fields.
- Compact holographic sound fields enable rapid one-step assembly of matter in 3D, Science Advances 9 (2023), https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.adf6182. Relevant to acoustic assembly; different physical coupling from photopolymerization.

## Stages

P0 DONE: reproduce exact finite phase model and trace ambiguity. Synthetic only.
P1 OPEN: derive admissible volume-support compositions and contacts from native Nyx constructions; no arbitrary physical geometry.
P2 OPEN: pin public DISH archive version, file hashes, units, measurement versus simulation provenance, licenses and field/coherence regimes. Do not equate imported data with cyclotomic addresses. Establish a typed import and preserve originals.
P3 OPEN: compare independently observed material response to declared structural targets (frustration, composition, chirality) only after specifying measurement calibration and a physical transfer. The three target fields are a corpus-derived research direction; the exact materials-treatise version must be checked before activation.
P4 OPEN: compute native capacitary overlap and Morcillien only once the admissible envelopes, calibrated centers, and required transport maps are available. Hold out observations for a real comparison; record unresolved fibres and defects. No Gaussian fitting or ad hoc imported averages as active Nyx instruments.

Stop if phase coherence, material-response data, calibration, or provenance is missing. A real device would also require independent engineering and safety review. No hardware control is implemented.
