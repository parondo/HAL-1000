# Instructions for AI agents and automated contributors

## Authority and scope

Nyx is the mathematical working framework. Use the maintainer's approved, versioned definitions. V2 is the reference baseline; migration to V4 is not assumed approved. Later results require explicit provenance and compatibility review. A library name, historical analogy or familiar notation is not a license to substitute an external operation.

Read the source and operator contract before implementing or executing an operation. If a necessary definition, calibration, version or bridge is missing, return STOP with the missing prerequisite. STOP is not false and must not be coerced into zero.

## Preserve types

- Keep observable O, H_perp and ideal representations separate. Bridges require approved typed Phi/Psi and explicit defect certificates.
- Distinguish exact rational ratios, modular residues, cyclotomic evaluations and finite projective representations. Do not identify their domains.
- Distinguish Galois sigma from braid sigma and the different Sigma thresholds.
- Distinguish support, capacity, contacts, and full Morcillien. Capacitary overlap alone is not full gauge correlation.
- Do not infer physical realizability from finite algebraic identities.
- Reason precedes language. A language corpus is not a substitute for an unbuilt tool or interface.

## Active mathematics

Use approved primitives, admissible envelopes, compositions and calibrations. Non-Plenum permits finite successive constructions, not an actually realized infinite totality. Non-False distinguishes undefined or out-of-gauge expressions from locally false propositions. Rejected classical statistical estimators and Haar averaging remain outside active tooling. External methods require explicit internal compatibility proof and approval. Finite enumeration verifies an approved construction; it does not introduce a probabilistic model.

## Experiment protocol

1. State the mathematical question before designing the representation.
2. Pin the source version, section, data provenance and contract.
3. Declare synthetic inputs and modeling choices.
4. Execute only defined operations in their admissible domains.
5. Record exact outputs, defects and STOP states; preserve ties and failures.
6. Verify independently. Distinguish QED, QEG and QEC; never silently promote a seal.
7. Preserve original data and earlier results. Work in a reviewable research branch. No merge or changes to normative definitions without approval.

Every operator needs both a human explanation and a structured contract: identifier, version, source, typed inputs/outputs, preconditions, composition, invariants, undefined cases and test evidence. Missing fields remain explicitly unresolved. This directory is exploratory; code execution does not constitute maintainer approval of an axiom or physical bridge.
